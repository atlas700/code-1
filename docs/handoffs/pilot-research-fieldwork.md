# G1 research — evidence reconstruction and business revisits

Prepared: 28 October 2026. Updated: 31 October 2026. Use for the approved Helmand grain and mixed-goods
pilot. Current counts and gaps are in the [research record](pilot-research-evidence.md).
This is a blank collection template; it records no completed interview or visit.

Use the [G1 evidence register](g1-evidence-register.md) to track reconstructed
interviews, observations, and workflow coverage. It now contains B001's partial
record; reserved anonymous codes and unanswered questions are not completed
evidence records. The [grain-wholesaler records research](grain-wholesaler-records-research.md)
supplies proposed fields for collecting complete transactions without asking
the founder to design every notebook field. These fields are literature-informed
collection guidance, not proof of what a particular business recorded.

The latest report is 50 interviewees and approximately 50 personally observed
businesses, recalled without original notes. First reconstruct only details the
founder can establish, label their source as recollection, and keep uncertain
dates/counts explicit. Use a revisit to fill gaps rather than inventing historical
entries or repeating questions already answered in the research record.

## At the visit

1. Choose a business you already know and arrange to watch a real purchase or
   delivery. Start with: “Show me your last purchase and what happened when the
   goods arrived.” Ask one follow-up at a time, using what the staff actually show.
2. Take notes in Pashto or English. Keep raw notes privately, outside the public
   repository. Use anonymous business and participant codes in shared summaries.
3. For each workflow below, mark whether you **saw it**, **were told about it**,
   or **could not establish it**. Record gaps for another visit. A delivery alone
   does not establish all of the required workflow coverage.

## After the visit

Keep participant and visit references so repeated visits do not inflate totals.
For the latest recalled interviews and observations, retain that source label;
reconstruct dates only where details can actually be established. Keep the date
of reconstruction distinct from the date of the original visit.

Share an anonymized summary: visit date, business type, goods, what you directly
watched, what staff explained, and the remaining gaps. Keep names, contacts,
transcripts, and raw financial records in the private notes. The summary can be
written in Pashto.

Update the research record with completed evidence. Review G1 against its
[20-interview/five-observation requirement](../agent-sized-build-plan.md#g1--observe-the-work-before-modelling-it),
per-business workflow coverage, terminology, and unresolved policy differences.
Researching sales or worker payments does not add them to the pilot's feature
scope. Task 14 remains blocked until G1 is satisfied.
