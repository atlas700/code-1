# Taste Skill

Source: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill

Installed project-locally on 31 October 2026 from commit
`12af471f627f866411b4bd785b19646d3672784c`.

`design-taste-frontend/`, matching the skill's declared name. This includes its
